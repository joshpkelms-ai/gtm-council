# Source: Jared Waxman -- The strategy deliverable now lives in repos

> LinkedIn post by [[expert-jared-waxman]]. Raw: `raw/Jared Waxman/1.md`.

## Summary

The strategy deliverable is moving from decks to repos. One working session for an Unusual Ventures portfolio company produced:

- A company-context file (industry, model, audiences) -- the doc every other doc reads first.
- A two-audience ICP with pains ranked from actual customer quotes.
- Six competitor dossiers rolled into one threat matrix.
- Positioning and a 10-component messaging library, built in dependency order.
- Voice rules extracted from their real posts.

Every claim is tagged [VERIFIED], [INFERRED], [ESTIMATED], or [UNAVAILABLE] -- "a blank beats a hallucination, every time." The payoff is not speed (a day vs a quarter) but compounding: the next AI tool, hire, or agency reads the repo and starts smart instead of starting over. "Strategy you can diff. Research you can trust. Marketing that compounds." A commenter reframes it: strategy becomes an asset, not an artifact.

## Key takeaways

- See [[framework-strategy-as-repo]]: structured, version-controlled GTM strategy as the deliverable.
- See [[concept-claim-tagging]]: tag every claim with confidence/provenance; blanks beat hallucinations.
- Build artifacts in dependency order, starting from a company-context file.

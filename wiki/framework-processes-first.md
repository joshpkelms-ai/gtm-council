# Framework: Processes first, systems second, people third

> Winning by Design's Growth Architecture, as carried and applied by [[expert-koen-stam]] (originally from Jacco van der Kooij, 2016). SaaS-native organisations stack their model **processes first, systems second, people third**. The growth engine runs on the system; people tune it. Orgs built around people replace people; orgs built on process scale.

## The core inversion

Most GTM orgs are built around people performance: hire a superstar, the superstar drives the number, hire more superstars; when one leaves, the number leaves with them and the culture becomes the person, not the system. Growth Architecture inverts the stack. Measure systems performance, not individual performance. "Processes are what make your best people scalable" -- the opposite of a demotion.

Koen saw it across six country teams: the markets winning had reps executing a common process; the markets struggling had a lone superstar carrying the number.

## The 2026 amplifier: documentation as the AI foundation

The framework predates AI but AI makes it non-negotiable. "An undocumented process fed into an AI system returns noise. A documented, structured process returns speed." Operators with systems in place are reporting 30-40% output gains from the same headcount; the cost of documentation (voice notes, structured prompts, AI drafting) has collapsed, so the only remaining operator job is the judgment of what is worth documenting.

This is the same thesis, from the process/enablement angle, as [[concept-foundations-before-automation]] (AI multiplies whatever foundation exists) and [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]] (prove the motion before you scale it). It is the documentation-layer expression of [[framework-gtm-brain]] and [[framework-strategy-as-repo]]: structured, version-able process is what an agent can actually act on.

## GTMcraft: the productised version

Koen's GTMcraft renders the framework as three layers pointing at one outcome (more ARR from what you already have):

1. **Living GTM playbooks** -- documented, multi-asset operating systems per motion (account prioritisation, warm outbound, deal progression, async selling, intent outbound, AI-SDR operating system, international scaling).
2. **Coaching** -- where a playbook on the shelf becomes a decision.
3. **Claude Projects, Cowork, and agents** -- the Sales Coaching System and Playbook Builder that hold the structure so the operator brings the judgment. See [[play-agentic-playbook-builder]].

# Adam Robinson

> CEO at Retention.com and [[tool-rb2b]]. Person-level website visitor identity. Known for bootstrapping to scale, a high-output LinkedIn founder brand, and a thesis that B2B contact data is now commoditised.

## Who he is

Founder and CEO of Retention.com and [[tool-rb2b]] (RB2B = website visitor identification, identifies 70-80% of website traffic at the person level). Building [[tool-moltsets]], an unlimited-contact-data play priced at $27/mo. Helps bootstrapped founders reach $10M ARR. Built the data asset with Tate and a former president of Apollo / VP product and growth at ZoomInfo.

## Core positions

- **B2B contact data is commoditised.** See [[concept-data-commoditisation]]. He is "selling sawdust": the data team and asset already serve three other profitable business lines, so the marginal cost of a contact is near zero. He prices below competitors' CAC and lets gross margin crush the field.
- **Distribution is the moat, not the data.** A stolen database is no threat because the moat is the founder brand, UGC, and an internal validation engine, not the rows themselves. Execution beats ownership.
- **Lean, autonomous, founder-owned businesses are the new playbook.** See [[concept-lean-autonomous-gtm]]. RB2B crossed $9M ARR while running near-autonomously.

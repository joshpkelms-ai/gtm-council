# Alon Even

> Writes a weekly GTM newsletter on Substack (aloneven.substack.com). Argues the buyer journey has moved upstream into AI conversations and dark social, where most GTM systems cannot see, and that brand, demand, and retention are one compounding signal system rather than three separate motions.

## Who he is

B2B SaaS / AI GTM advisor and newsletter author. Publishes weekly.

## Core positions

- **The buyer journey moved upstream.** Buyers discover vendors via AI (ChatGPT), then Google the brand only to verify. By the time they enter pipeline the decision is largely made. See [[concept-ai-first-buyer-journey]].
- **Signal lives in three places before any search:** peer / dark social signal (private Slacks, DMs), AI signal (what ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity/Gemini surface), and verification signal (your site, LinkedIn, customer proof). AEO is necessary but incomplete without real signal behind it.
- **Start with how buyers actually buy,** not a persona deck: who's in the room, what triggers the search, who they ask first, where they verify. Then build a system that updates as the journey shifts.
- **Brand + demand + retention = one compounding system.** Brand builds memory pre-market, demand captures intent, retention creates the proof the next buyer hears from peers. Treated separately, they leak.

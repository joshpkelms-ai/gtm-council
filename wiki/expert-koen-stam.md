# Koen Stam

> Author of GTMcraft: The Future GTM Operator (koenstam.substack.com) and Director International at Personio; co-leads the Winning by Design Growth Institute chapter for the Netherlands. Writes at the intersection of go-to-market practice and AI-powered systems for founders scaling 0 to 10M+ ARR. GTMcraft is his side practice, built on 100,000+ indexed GTM signals.

## Who he is

A 13-year GTM operator who learned the Winning by Design Customer Centric Communication framework from Jacco van der Kooij in 2016 and has been productising the instinct to document, systematise, and coach ever since. GTMcraft is the named version of the "Saturday work" he was doing in Berlin a decade ago.

## Core positions

- **Processes first, systems second, people third.** The Winning by Design Growth Architecture: orgs that build around people replace people; orgs that build processes first scale. Most companies at 5-25M ARR have a systems problem, not a headcount problem. See [[framework-processes-first]].
- **Documentation is the foundation AI amplifies.** "An undocumented process fed into an AI system returns noise. A documented, structured process returns speed." The cost of documentation has never been lower (voice notes, structured prompts, AI drafting); the operator's job is the judgment of what is worth documenting. Sibling of [[concept-foundations-before-automation]].
- **More ARR from what you already have.** The 2026 bet is better systems and sharper coaching, not more reps; AI agents scan the noise so the operator can focus on decisions.

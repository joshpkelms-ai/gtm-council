# Source: Rippling's Pioneers Building a GTM Engine From Scratch

> The Signal interview by [[expert-brendan-short]] with [[expert-brandon-camhi]] (COO, Netic; ex-Rippling, scaled marketing to ~$1B ARR) and [[expert-noah-adelstein]] (Head of Growth, Netic; ex-Rippling). Raw: see frontmatter. Anchors [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]]; feeds [[concept-specialist-moat]], [[concept-hybrid-gtm]], [[concept-foundations-before-automation]].

## Summary

- **The expected story was "more agents than humans"; the actual thesis was the opposite.** Two operators who scaled Rippling to near-$1B are rebuilding at Netic with every AI advantage, and their takeaway is that the foundation matters more than the tooling. Collecting learnings is a human job; the perfectly built agent cannot scale something before a human with taste collects the context first.
- **Alpha is in timeless marketing principles, not new tactics.** The tactical playbook (events, signals, storytelling) is broadly unchanged; what has shifted is that the "permission to play" quality bar rises every day. Returns accrue to teams that tell incredible stories, not to whoever owns the best agent. "AI raises the bar for execution."
- **Hire spiky mutants, not generalists.** A small team where each person is exceptional at one dimension and raises the bar there, instead of a 100+ person org. Netic's five pillars: Growth, Content, GTM Engineering, Events, Product Marketing. Feeds [[concept-specialist-moat]].
- **Know where to seek alpha and where to do no harm.** Build secret sauce in some dimensions (a creative, deep TAM build); choose predictable reliability in others (Salesforce over an AI-native CRM, for the integration ecosystem). Foundations: Salesforce, [[tool-gong]]. Build-on-top: Replit, [[tool-claude-code]], [[tool-clay]] (lead orchestration, inbound enrichment, signals).
- **SDRs aren't dead; dumb SDRs are.** Netic's two senior SDRs run a weekly "Shark Tank" loop with Noah: surface signals, align account-selection criteria, review live emails and responses, bake it back into next week. SDRs generate the context and playbook the GTMEs later codify.
- **Earn the right to automate.** "Go prove the ceiling is sufficiently high before you think about all the automation." Most outbound is bad because people scaled the C+ version with AI -- automated mediocrity. Brandon: "I would take a totally analog GTM org that produces hyper-relevant things over a fully automated one that isn't working. AI is a means to an end." Anchors [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]].
- **Every exec must be in the tools.** Brandon went from a ~200-person org to personally coding marketing assets; leaders not operating inside the tools will fall behind.
- **Pick the right company.** The best PMF signal is revenue traction with nothing built in GTM yet; magic is an incredible product/market/founder paired with top GTM creativity and execution.

## Key takeaways

- The "earn the right to automate" sequencing is the manual-proof-first sibling of [[concept-foundations-before-automation]] (Moscatiello) and [[framework-processes-first]] (Stam); all three reach "validate before you scale".
- "Spiky mutants" is the human-hiring expression of [[concept-specialist-moat]] (be the Olympist) and pairs with [[concept-hybrid-gtm]] (humans at the centre, AI as means).
- The Shark Tank loop is a concrete context-generation engine -- the human upstream of any codified playbook ([[framework-ai-employee-loop]]'s context step, [[concept-context-assembly]]).

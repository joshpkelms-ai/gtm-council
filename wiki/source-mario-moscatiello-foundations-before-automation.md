# Source: Mario Moscatiello -- Foundations Before Automation (S2E5)

## Summary

- **Foundations before automation is the real step change.** GTM engineering is a step change only with PMF in place and clean revops data. AI multiplies whatever foundation exists -- bad data equals bad signal equals bad results, amplified. Two foundations: the PMM hat (personas, competitor, market, what works) and the revops hat (clean fields, enrichments, attribution). Pre-PMF teams hacking GTM with AI "just create more damage faster". Direct echo of Sachin Jha's [[framework-three-layer-gtm]].
- **Owning workflows end-to-end is the leverage unlock.** Paid SEO goes idea -> keyword research -> published post in an hour; an SDR manager goes list -> copy -> launched campaign in the same hour. Framing: force multiplier per role, not headcount replacement -- "five strong people doing the work of fifteen, not one star doing the work of ten" (key-man risk). Anthropic and OpenAI hire engineers AND AEs while shipping the tools that supposedly replace both.
- **Warm outbound is signal triangulation.** The pipeline-growth doubling came from two plays, not cold blasts. (1) Events: leads into [[tool-common-room]], rank by product signup / open-source repo use, SDRs call only the warm subset. (2) PLG signups: reach the engineer who signed up, prospect decision-makers in the same org, warm them with ads before the SDR call (Twilio "Ask Your Developer" at scale). ABM at Series B is defensible if you know your audience.
- **Hire barrels, not ammunition; outsource deep expertise.** Generalists who run a project end-to-end without a playbook beat narrow specialists (his best SDR sold pest control door-to-door). Four traits: agency, curiosity, taste, chip on the shoulder. AI drives copy/code cost to zero, so taste differentiates. Complement with agencies (deliverability, paid media, scaled outbound) so the team manages agents and workflows.
- **One habit to start:** a Gong digest of what customers say -- stop doom-scrolling and copying other people's posts.

## Key takeaways

- "AI is a multiplier of garbage too" is the same thesis as foundation-before-activation; cross-attributed in [[concept-foundations-before-automation]].
- Warm outbound via signal triangulation is a concrete instance of signal-led targeting and [[concept-signal-vs-intent]] (who is moving and why), executed through Common Room.
- Barrels-not-ammunition and "taste differentiates" are the human-side input to [[concept-specialist-moat]] and [[concept-hybrid-gtm]].

## Core positions

- **Fix the context before the skill.** Most teams obsess over running skills and ignore what the agent reads first. If context is stitched together at query time from disagreeing sources, a better prompt cannot save it; you are automating bad judgment at scale. Assemble and unify context upstream, then let the agent read one clean view. See [[concept-context-assembly]].
- **Optimise for AI citations, not just Google rankings.** Buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini before Google; that traffic never shows in GA. Sites that read well to humans but look unstructured to an LLM do not get cited. See [[concept-aeo]].
- **Extract structure the moment it is fresh.** Source material for case studies and stories decays weekly; run a structured extraction at the moment of the call, with placeholders for anything unsourced. See [[play-case-study-extraction]].
- **Client acquisition is a compounding channel stack.** Network, then referrals, then an owned audience (LinkedIn), then community; each layer carries the next. See [[concept-compounding-channel-stack]].

# Concept: Be the Olympist (specialist depth as the moat)

> [[expert-guillaume-cabane]]: when AI commoditises execution, the durable human advantage is world-class depth in a narrow field -- "be the Olympist of your field." [[expert-mario-moscatiello]] supplies the near-term version: with the cost of copy and code driven to zero, **taste** is what differentiates.

## The idea

There will not be hundreds of millions of Python developers. There will be a few thousand master-level specialists who can beat the best LLM in their narrow domain. The path for younger talent: pick a niche, dedicate ten years (roughly age 15 to 25), become world-class -- "most Olympians are out of college by 25; why would tech be different." Depth, not breadth, is what stays scarce.

## The near-term face: taste

Moscatiello's hiring lens makes it concrete now. AI brings the cost of writing copy and code to zero, so the differentiator is taste and a deep understanding of who you are selling to. His four traits for great GTM hires -- **agency** (do the thing, don't tell me you'll plan to), **curiosity** (the playbooks that worked five years ago don't work now), **taste**, and a **chip on the shoulder** -- describe the person who supplies the judgment AI cannot. This is "hire barrels, not ammunition" (Keith Rabois / Vinod Khosla): generalists who own a project end-to-end without a playbook.

[[expert-brandon-camhi]] (ex-Rippling, now Netic) gives the team-design version: **hire spiky mutants, not generalists** -- "a small team of mutants who each are spiky in one or more areas," each raising the bar on a different dimension, instead of scaling a 100+ person org. AI automates the commodity work, so you want people amazing at a few things and build automation on top of their output. See [[source-brendan-short-rippling-netic-earn-to-automate]].

## Why it matters here

It is the answer to "what do humans do once agents execute?" -- and the reason [[concept-hybrid-gtm]] holds: the human contributes scarce depth and taste, the agent contributes scaled execution. It also reframes defensibility: when SaaS-for-SaaS gets vibe-coded away, individual mastery (and arguably skills as IP) is where value concentrates. The counterpoint to a pure [[concept-borrow-from-engineering]] reading.

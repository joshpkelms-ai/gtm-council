# Source: Adam Robinson -- RB2B hit $9M ARR, 15 insane facts

> LinkedIn post by [[expert-adam-robinson]]. Raw: `raw/Adam Robinson/2.md`.

## Summary

[[tool-rb2b]] crossed $9M ARR while running near-autonomously. Robinson lists 15 facts: the founders went all-in on [[tool-moltsets]] five weeks ago; Tate has not touched RB2B code in five weeks; Robb handles escalated tickets in under 15 min/day; Adam posts 3x/week on LinkedIn, not about RB2B.

How it works: the product was kept dead simple so AI could handle customer interaction; world-class documentation lets Intercom Fin resolve 98.9% of tickets (up from 25%), escalating 1.1%; the LinkedIn thought-leadership brand is a cheap acquisition channel; being a data company with two other product lines means zero marginal cost per contact, so they priced a zero-friction PLG product below competitors' CAC. Bootstrapped, they were happy with a sub-VC-scale ($10-15M) outcome, so they stayed SMB freemium PLG at 9%+ monthly churn rather than chasing mid-market. Competitors left for bigger, more complex things; RB2B is "the last man standing" with a powerfully simple value prop (RB2B = website ID).

## Key takeaways

- The new playbook is "less, smarter, faster, and completely yours": founder-led, bootstrapped, sub-VC-scale, and near-autonomous past $10M ARR. See [[concept-lean-autonomous-gtm]].
- Simplicity is a design choice that enables AI support deflection (Intercom Fin at 98.9%) and cheap ops.
- A founder brand is an acquisition channel; cross-subsidised data crushes competitors on gross margin.

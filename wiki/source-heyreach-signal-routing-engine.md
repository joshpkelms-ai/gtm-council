# Source: HeyReach -- Signal-based outbound: a safe, scalable routing engine for LinkedIn

> The execution-side blueprint of [[framework-sales-orchestration]], by [[expert-nada-komnenic]] on the HeyReach blog. Raw: `raw/Signal-based outbound...md`.

## Summary

Every day the GTM stack fires signals nonstop (funding, job changes, tech installs, enrichment updates, CRM scores). Each looks like a high-intent lead; together they are noise. "Most outbound teams don't have a signal problem. They have an orchestration problem." When multiple signals fire for one prospect with no logic layer between them, you get duplicate outreach, conflicting messages, overloaded sender seats, and rising account risk; this is where trigger-based automation breaks.

The answer is a unified orchestration layer that sits *before* LinkedIn execution and controls how signals are processed before they reach a sender. The minimum engine, six steps:

1. **Validate** -- is the signal complete, accurate, usable?
2. **Dedupe** -- is another workflow or sender already engaging this account?
3. **Prioritise** -- which signal actually matters right now?
4. **Route** -- which sender, seat, or playbook fits this lead?
5. **Pace** -- can the LinkedIn accounts safely handle this volume today? (sender health / deliverability)
6. **Monitor** -- is the system behaving, or quietly failing at scale?

The payoff is high-volume outbound that stays controlled and effective without sacrificing sender health or delivery.

## Key takeaways

- Signal-based outbound triggers on real-time buying signals, not static lists, but it fails without an orchestration layer.
- Validate / Dedupe / Prioritise / Route / Pace / Monitor is the routing engine that keeps volume safe.
- Pacing protects sender health; Monitor is the guard against silent failure at scale.

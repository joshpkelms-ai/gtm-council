# Eric Nowoslawski

> Founder of Growth Engine X, Clay Enterprise Partner (LinkedIn handle "outboundphd"). Runs a high-volume cold-email agency on a tiny headcount by treating coding agents ([[tool-codex]], [[tool-claude-code]]) as trainable employees. The corpus's loudest voice on cost discipline and agentic execution in outbound.

## Who he is

Founder of Growth Engine X, a cold-email agency, and a Clay Enterprise Partner. His thesis: a small team plus well-trained agents outproduces a large team. He claims Growth Engine X cut headcount in half (down to ~7 people) while sending more email and running more campaigns, because agents now respond to leads, clean ICP lists, build TAMs, produce client reports, analyse campaigns and propose the next one. Posts hands-on, opinionated build notes; widely read by Clay-certified operators and GTM engineers.

## Core positions

- **Treat AI like a new hire, not a magic box.** It takes ~3x the effort to train, but once trained it never stops, never takes time off, never leaves for a competitor. The failure mode is handing it instructions and expecting it to "figure it out". See [[framework-ai-employee-loop]].
- **Context, connection, creation.** Three things to solve, in order: dump your written record and call recordings into a company brain; get every API key wired up; then build a loop where the agent spots work and proposes a plan. See [[framework-ai-employee-loop]] and the company-brain family in [[framework-gtm-brain]].
- **Make every paid API and AI call justify itself.** Free deterministic steps first, in-plan sub-agents next, paid batch API only for the remainder. See [[concept-deterministic-first]] and [[play-cheap-list-processing]].
- **The list is the message.** Segment by who a company sells to, not just what it is; if a data point would change what you say, split the campaign. See [[concept-list-is-the-message]].
- **Lock the CTA, give every data point upfront.** When an agent optimises a campaign, never let it make a "game-time decision" about what to pull or what counts as a win. See [[concept-locked-cta]].
- **Clarity on the end state is the remaining human skill.** With agentic goal runs, the work is defining done precisely and verifying it. See [[play-agentic-goal-run]].

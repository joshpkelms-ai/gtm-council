# Concept: The AI-first buyer journey

> [[expert-alon-even]]: AI shapes buyer preference upstream, before buyers ever enter your pipeline or even search your brand. Discovery happens in AI conversations and peer networks; the Google search is now just a verification step. Most GTM systems still optimise for what happens after the hand is raised.

## The shift

A buyer finds a vendor on ChatGPT, Googles the brand to check it's legit, then converts. The campaign didn't drive discovery; AI did. The journey didn't disappear, it moved upstream into places the GTM system can't see. Cited evidence: 51% of B2B software buyers now start research in an AI chatbot (up from 29% a year earlier); 72% start with peer recommendations in private groups; 92% enter formal evaluation with at least one vendor already in mind; 70% of B2B tech queries trigger AI Overviews, mostly with no click-out.

## Where presence must live (three signals)

- **Peer / dark social signal** -- what buyers hear from networks, communities, colleagues in private Slacks and DMs; invisible to attribution, weighs most because it comes from someone already trusted.
- **AI signal** -- what ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Mode surface; they learn from expert content, research, reviews, and community, so if you're not in those sources you're not in the answers. This is the demand side of [[concept-aeo]].
- **Verification signal** -- what appears when someone Googles your brand after hearing about you: site, LinkedIn, customer proof. Confirms interest rather than creating it.

## AEO is necessary but incomplete

Optimising for AI Overviews (schema, citation-friendly content, structured data) isn't wrong, but "you can't earn a presence inside a journey that already happened without you" without real signal behind it. AEO ([[concept-aeo]]) is the mechanics; this concept is the demand-side reason it matters, plus the warning that mechanics alone are hollow.

## What to do

Start with how and why buyers actually buy -- the real process (who's in the room, what triggers the search, who they ask first, where they verify), not a persona deck. Then build a system that moves when buyers do, watching how the journey shifts and updating where signals get created. "AI didn't change what builds trust. It changed where trust is encountered, and how fast your GTM system needs to adapt."

## One compounding system

Brand builds memory before buyers are in-market, demand captures intent, retention and expansion create the proof the next buyer hears from peers. Treated as three separate motions they leak; as one system they compound. Connects to [[concept-compounding-channel-stack]].

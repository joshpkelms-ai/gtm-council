# Play: Structured case-study extraction

> The instant an engagement closes, run a skill that turns the call transcript into a sourced case-study scaffold, with placeholders for anything missing, before the source material decays. [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]]'s motion against the eight-week case-study graveyard.

## Why timing is the play

Source material decays weekly. Week 1 the verbatim quote is fresh and the metrics are in last week's QBR; week 8 the recording is buried and the writer invents "50% faster" that nobody can source. Extract within ~48 hours or you are writing fiction.

## The motion

1. **Trigger on close.** When the engagement closes (or the relevant call lands), feed the [[tool-granola]] transcript to the skill.
2. **Extract structure, not prose.** The skill pulls:
   - a **quote library** -- 3-5 verbatim quotes with attribution;
   - a **metrics table** where every number traces to a source;
   - a flagged **signature moment** -- the specific thing the customer said that makes the story feel real.
3. **Placeholder, never invent.** Anything missing gets `[METRIC NEEDED]` or `[PLACEHOLDER]`. The draft never ships with invented numbers. This is [[concept-claim-tagging]] applied to content.

## Result

Transcript to sourced draft in about a minute, versus 4-6 hours and weeks of meandering edits. The output is a scaffold a writer finishes, not a finished piece.

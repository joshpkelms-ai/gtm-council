# Source: Adam Robinson -- MoltSets, unlimited contact data at $27/mo

> LinkedIn post by [[expert-adam-robinson]] announcing [[tool-moltsets]]. Raw: `raw/Adam Robinson/1.md`.

## Summary

Robinson rails against data vendors pricing the standard at $99 per 5,000 credits and launches MoltSets at $27/mo for unlimited contact data, claiming coverage and accuracy on par with ZoomInfo and Apollo (built by him, Tate, and a former Apollo president / ZoomInfo VP). He can price this low because he is "selling sawdust": the same data team and asset already power three other profitable business lines, so marginal cost is near zero.

On theft: he is unworried. A stolen database changes nothing because the moat is distribution (his founder brand, UGC, internal validation engine), not the rows. He cites 5x5 as having already commoditised B2B data and wants to trigger "an explosion of vibe-coded apps" by making contact data nearly free.

## Key takeaways

- Contact data is a commodity; price it like one. See [[concept-data-commoditisation]].
- Distribution beats data as a moat; "execution is becoming the moat" (echoed by a commenter, Adam Behr: reduce the distance between signal and execution).
- Cross-subsidised data asset lets a zero-marginal-cost product undercut competitors' CAC.

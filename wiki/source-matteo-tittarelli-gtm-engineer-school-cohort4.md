# Source: Matteo Tittarelli -- GTM Engineer School Cohort 4 muse-company call

> LinkedIn post by [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]]. Raw: `raw/Matteo Titt/6.md`. Establishes GTM Engineer School, co-founded with [[expert-jared-waxman]].

## Summary

A call for "muse companies" for Cohort 4 (May 25 - July 14) of GTM Engineer School, the school Matteo co-founded with [[expert-jared-waxman]]. The cohort is project-based: every student picks one real GTM problem, builds an end-to-end solution over 8 weeks, and validates it with a real "muse" company and stakeholder who gives feedback as it is built.

A muse company agrees to a 30-minute Week 1 intake call, 2-3 feedback sessions (30 min each) across the 8 weeks, and honest feedback. In return it gets a custom-built workflow free to keep, access to a GTME who knows the problem deeply, and a first look at a potential hire. Problem areas span the GTM surface: outbound, account research, AE enablement, lead scoring, lifecycle, RFPs. Framed explicitly as a student-led build with a real stakeholder, not consulting or a guaranteed deliverable.

## Key takeaways

- GTM Engineer School is a cohort-based, project-led programme co-founded by Matteo Tittarelli and Jared Waxman (see [[expert-jared-waxman]]).
- Pedagogy: build one real workflow end-to-end against a real stakeholder ("muse company"), with customer discovery and feedback.
- Aligns with the school's repo-and-build ethos in [[framework-strategy-as-repo]].

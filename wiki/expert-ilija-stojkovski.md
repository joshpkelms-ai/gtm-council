# Ilija Stojkovski

> Writes for the HeyReach blog on sales orchestration. Argues that GTM coordination is an engineering problem, not a management one: "Sales isn't broken, it's unorchestrated."

## Who he is

A HeyReach contributor (see [[tool-heyreach]]). One source in the corpus, the foundational essay on [[framework-sales-orchestration]].

## Core positions

- **Coordination is an engineering problem.** More meetings, checklists, and required CRM fields make it worse. Engineer the flow instead so every lead follows the same proven path. See [[framework-sales-orchestration]].
- **Flow over silo; automate the decision.** Speed-to-lead is decisive (5-minute response = ~8x conversion); reps sell only ~28% of the time, the rest is coordination chaos. Design for the end-to-end journey with cross-functional triggers (SDR <-> AE <-> CSM) and agent-driven decisions.

# Source: The GTM Mistake I Made for Years Before Berlin Fixed It

> Newsletter essay by [[expert-koen-stam]] (GTMcraft; Director International, Personio). Raw: see frontmatter. Anchors [[framework-processes-first]]; provides [[play-agentic-playbook-builder]]; feeds [[concept-foundations-before-automation]] and [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]].

## Summary

- **The lesson (Berlin, 2016):** taught the Winning by Design Customer Centric Communication framework (from Jacco van der Kooij) to six country leaders and 60+ colleagues. On unpaid Saturdays he built outbound playbooks, discovery checklists, coaching frameworks, onboarding decks -- the instinct to take messy lived experience and turn it into systems other people can use. That instinct, not authority, was the asset; it compounded into a 10-year career.
- **The principle: processes first, systems second, people third.** Winning by Design's Growth Architecture. Orgs built around people replace people when the superstar leaves; orgs built on process scale. Measure systems performance, not individuals. Anchors [[framework-processes-first]].
- **The 2026 amplifier:** "An undocumented process fed into an AI system returns noise. A documented, structured process returns speed." Operators with systems in place report 30-40% output gains from the same headcount; documentation has never been cheaper (voice notes, structured prompts, AI drafting), so the only operator job left is the judgment of what is worth documenting.
- **The bet:** most orgs at 5-25M ARR have a systems problem, not a headcount problem -- "more ARR from what you already have". AEs who could close 20% more with sharper coaching; SDRs who could book 30% more with a tighter playbook.
- **GTMcraft = the productised instinct,** three layers to one outcome: (1) living GTM playbooks (7 live, each a 28-asset suite); (2) group and 1-1 coaching ("a playbook in a coaching session is a change"); (3) Claude Projects, Cowork, and agents.
- **Two runnable Claude Projects (full prompts in raw):**
  - **Sales Coaching System** -- analyses call transcripts (connect [[tool-gong]] or Leexi), scores SPICED or MEDDICCC per element, cites specific transcript moments with rewrite suggestions, and tracks each coachee's development across sessions so coaching compounds per rep.
  - **GTM Playbook Builder** -- turns raw operator notes into an execution-ready playbook on a fixed 3-phase (CREATE / OPERATIONALISE / SCALE) 12-step architecture, with an AI-layer and scaling-guidance line per step and a banned-language list (no em dashes, no hype words). See [[play-agentic-playbook-builder]].

## Key takeaways

- The same "documentation is the foundation AI amplifies" thesis as [[concept-foundations-before-automation]] (Moscatiello) and the validate-before-scale stance of [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]] (Camhi/Adelstein) -- this source is the process/enablement angle on the family.
- The Playbook Builder is a copyable structured-prompt instance of documentation-as-AI-foundation; cousin of [[play-case-study-extraction]] (structure first, human supplies real specifics).
- Scope note: SPICED/MEDDICCC coaching brushes the out-of-scope discovery-methodology line; kept for the in-scope thrust (documentation-first systems, AI-amplified GTM ops), with the methodology recorded but not expanded -- mirrors the Renaissance of Enablement decision.

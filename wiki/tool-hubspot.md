# Tool: HubSpot

> The CRM / marketing platform that recurs across this corpus as the system of record agents read from and write to: lifecycle, deals, notes, pipeline, and contacts.

## What it is

A CRM and marketing platform. In the agentic GTM stack it is typically the owner of CRM truth (lifecycle, deals, notes, company traits, pipeline movement), one of several sources a GTM agent must route to. High agent readiness in [[tool-gtm-docs-registry]] (official MCP, CLI, API, OpenAPI, llms.txt, SDK).

## Position in the corpus

- **Audited for ad strategy.** [[expert-andy-toizer]]'s skill audited 112k HubSpot contacts and built 9 segmented ad audiences in 10 minutes. See [[play-crm-to-ad-strategy]] and [[source-andy-toizer-crm-ad-strategy-skill]].
- **A source in the GTM Brain.** HubSpot owns CRM in the source-routing map. See [[framework-gtm-brain]].
- **The governed write target.** Practitioners trust a rule-aware [[tool-clay]] table to write to HubSpot rather than a raw agent. See [[play-agentic-prospect-clay-crm-push]].

# Shashank Khanna

> Founder in Residence and Head of GTM Engineering at Vanta. Originator of the Vanta/[[tool-dust]] motion: democratise agent-building across the whole company, then let real adoption tell the GTM Engineering team which workflows deserve production-grade investment. Documented by [[expert-noah-adelstein]].

## Who he is

GTM engineering leader supporting a much larger sales and marketing org, facing the universal GTME problem: infinite workflows to automate, finite engineering hours. His answer turned the prioritisation question into an adoption-measurement question.

## Core positions

- **Adoption is the discovery engine.** Rather than building from leadership ideas or GTME hunches alone, let reps build their own agents in [[tool-dust]] and watch which spread. "Run many small experiments across the company, see which ones worked, and then focus engineering time on the workflows with the clearest demand." See [[concept-adoption-as-prioritisation]].
- **Invest in the data foundation first.** Agents are only as good as the data they read. One source-of-truth fields, no stale data, and custom MCPs built on top of Gong and Snowflake to gate access and clean out garbage; document how the data is structured so agents interpret it correctly. The unglamorous work that makes everything else possible.
- **Celebrate the builders.** Enablement plus public recognition (the CRO's weekly shoutouts) activates the early adopters who lead AI experimentation.

# Source: Wesley Hoang -- One sentence builds a lead list (Claude + AI Ark via MCP)

> LinkedIn post plus setup transcript by [[expert-wesley-hoang]]. Raw: `raw/Wesley Hoang/1.md`.

## Summary

Building a lead list used to take an hour in a database; now it takes one sentence. Cymate connected [[tool-claude-code]] to [[tool-ai-ark]] (referred to in the transcript as AIoG) via [[tool-mcp]]. Instead of filters and dropdowns, you describe your ICP in plain English: "Grab me 50 CEOs in New York running SaaS companies doing one to five million ARR." Claude runs the search through AI Ark, pulls the data, and exports a CSV with emails and LinkedIn profiles. You can waterfall it: have Claude pull the list, score every lead, and flag the ones worth reaching out to first. One conversation, no tab switching, no manual filtering.

The transcript walks through connecting via a custom connector in Claude settings (Connectors > Add custom connector), pasting the AI Ark MCP URL, and inserting an API key generated in AI Ark settings. Once live, AI Ark appears in Claude and you prospect by describing criteria; the more detail on ICP, the better the list.

## Key takeaways

- See [[play-natural-language-icp-list-build]]: describe ICP, agent builds and scores the list, exports CSV.
- See [[concept-agentic-sourcing]]: MCP collapses UI filtering into conversation.
- Scoring and flagging in the same conversation prioritises outreach without leaving the agent.

# Guillaume Cabane

> Founder and general partner of HyperGrowth Partners, working hands-on with Ramp, Neon, Zapier, AirOps, n8n, and Attention. The recognised "godfather of GTM engineering" -- pioneered the Clearbit reveal loop, personalised outbound, and experimentation-as-system before any of it was standard. Formerly VP Growth at Drift, Segment, and Gorgias, and interim CMO at Ramp through its $10M to $1B+ ARR run. Guest on the GTM Engineer School podcast (S2E7), hosted by [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]]. (HyperGrowth Partners is where Matteo and [[expert-jared-waxman]] met.)

## Core positions

- **Hybrid GTM beats full automation.** He has yet to see a scale-up operate without a GTM team. He tested full automation against a human+AI hybrid at Ramp and failed to beat the hybrid baseline; others brought their prompts and also failed. SDR/BDR teams are still in active hiring across his portfolio. The corpus's strongest empirical counterweight to the automation hype. Anchors [[concept-hybrid-gtm]].
- **True AI adoption stays under 1%.** Most marketing teams have done nothing beyond a GPT prompt or two; shown the output, they like it but won't learn to build it. The technologists who compound value with AI are a tiny fraction, and the gap widens every quarter -- two societies splitting around AI.
- **Response rates collapse when every bot acts in its owner's name.** As automated outreach saturates every channel, the channel-level returns fall -- the structural risk under volume-first automation.
- **SaaS-for-SaaS is the risky bet; tech-for-non-tech stays safe.** Build-versus-buy is shifting back to build; niche subscriptions are hard to justify when you can vibe-code the same thing in an hour. Software sold to other software companies is most exposed (LLMs buy for them, or enterprises build in-house). Selling tech to non-tech buyers (Ramp) stays safe. Touches "are skills the new IP" and where defensibility goes.
- **Be the Olympist of your field.** The future holds not hundreds of millions of Python developers but a few thousand master-level specialists who can beat the best LLM in a narrow domain. Pick a niche, dedicate ten years (15 to 25), become world-class -- most Olympians are out of college by 25. The durable moat when AI commoditises execution. Anchors [[concept-specialist-moat]].

# Concept: Compounding channel stack

> Client and pipeline acquisition is not one channel but a stack you add to each year, where the channels you own and invest in eventually carry the rest. [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]]'s read on four years of solopreneur growth.

## The layers

1. **Personal network** -- where it starts (his Year 1: ~85%). People you have already worked with, requiring no audience or system.
2. **Referrals** -- the network starts recommending you; compounds on top (Year 2: ~30%).
3. **Owned audience** -- posting on LinkedIn/X turns into your first channel you control outright, with no intro to wait on (Year 3: ~40% LinkedIn).
4. **Community** -- earning into the right operator community (his was HyperGrowth Partners) brings the highest-quality clients, and often more: it gave him a co-founder ([[expert-jared-waxman]]) and the push to build his own community.

## The lesson

Each wave of clients came from somewhere new, and the later channels only worked because of years invested earlier. Where to start over: list everyone you loved working with; build the personal brand before you need it; use it to get into a community fast. Run together, they compound; it took four years to stack all three, but it could be done in one.

# Tool: Framer

> Website builder whose pages are "AEO-ready by default", and which ships a free AEO Scanner that scores any site for how well LLMs can parse and cite it. The corpus's reference tool for [[concept-aeo]].

## What it is

A no-code website/CMS builder. Its relevance here is answer-engine optimisation: Framer pages ship with clean HTML, fast load times, structured metadata, and auto-updating sitemaps, so they are parseable by LLMs by default (its CMS 3.0 update pushed this further).

## The AEO Scanner

A free tool (works on any site, not just Framer-hosted): paste a URL to get an AEO score, the specific issues hurting visibility, and a fix plan. Scored dimensions in the example: Findable, Quotable, Understandable, Trustworthy. Flags issues like missing schema, broken header hierarchy, missing internal links, and content the AI cannot parse.

## Position in the corpus

[[expert-matteo-tittarelli]] (a brand partner) uses the Scanner to make the AEO case: optimise for AI citations, not just Google rankings. See [[source-matteo-tittarelli-aeo-framer-scanner]].

# Concept: Foundations before automation

> [[expert-mario-moscatiello]] (VP Marketing, Airbyte): GTM engineering is a step change only once the foundations are in place. AI is a multiplier of whatever foundation exists -- including garbage. "Bad data equals bad signal equals bad results, and AI in the mix is a multiplier effect." Pre-PMF teams hacking GTM with AI "just create more damage faster".

## The two foundations

1. **The PMM hat:** personas, competitor and market understanding, and a clear read on what actually works.
2. **The revops hat:** clean fields, clean enrichments, clean attribution.

Without both, more activity equals more noise -- automation scales the noise. This is the same thesis, from a different author, as [[expert-sachin-jha]]'s [[framework-three-layer-gtm]] (product marketing -> context engineering -> GTM engineering; build in order or amplify garbage) and the foundation-first message of [[concept-context-assembly]] (fix it at the data layer, not the prompt).

## Why it belongs as its own concept

It is the independently-arrived-at, empirically-framed version of foundation-before-activation: stated by an operator who doubled a company's pipeline-growth rate, and grounded in "AI as multiplier" rather than a layered model. Cross-attributed rather than folded so the Jha and Moscatiello statements each keep their source.

## The sibling family

Three authors reach the same conclusion from different angles, each kept as its own page for attribution:

- **This page (Moscatiello):** AI multiplies whatever foundation exists, including garbage.
- **[[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]] (Camhi / Adelstein):** prove the motion manually before you scale it; automating mediocrity just makes more noise.
- **[[framework-processes-first]] (Stam):** processes first, systems second, people third; documentation is the foundation AI amplifies, undocumented process returns noise.

## The corollary: leverage per role

Once foundations exist, the unlock is that every team member can own a workflow idea-to-live (paid SEO in an hour; an SDR manager from list to launched campaign in an hour). The framing is **force multiplier per role, not headcount replacement** -- "five strong people doing the work of fifteen, not one star doing the work of ten" -- which feeds [[concept-hybrid-gtm]].

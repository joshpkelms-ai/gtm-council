# Tool: MoltSets

> Unlimited B2B contact data at $27/mo, from [[expert-adam-robinson]]'s team. A deliberate commoditisation play against ZoomInfo and Apollo.

## What it is

A contact-data product priced at $27/mo for unlimited contact data, against an industry standard of ~$99 per 5,000 credits. Built by Adam Robinson, Tate, and a former president of Apollo / VP product and growth at ZoomInfo. Coverage and accuracy claimed on par with incumbents (it has to be good enough to serve [[tool-rb2b]]).

## The thesis

A pure [[concept-data-commoditisation]] play. The data team and asset already serve three other profitable business lines, so the marginal cost of a contact is near zero ("selling sawdust"). Pricing it as a near-free commodity is a distribution play: Robinson is unworried about theft because the moat is brand, UGC, and an internal validation engine, not the data. He expects it to fuel "an explosion of vibe-coded apps."

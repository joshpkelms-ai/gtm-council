# Source: Guillaume Cabane -- Be the Olympist of Your Field (S2E7)

> GTM Engineer School podcast, S2E7, hosted by [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]], guest [[expert-guillaume-cabane]] (founder, HyperGrowth Partners). Raw: `raw/Clippings/S2E7 "Be the Olympist of Your Field"  Guillaume Cabane.md`. The corpus's strongest counterweight to the full-automation narrative. Anchors [[concept-hybrid-gtm]] and [[concept-specialist-moat]].

## Summary

- **Hybrid GTM beats full automation.** He has yet to see a scale-up run without a GTM team. He tested full automation against a human+AI hybrid at Ramp and failed to beat the hybrid; others brought prompts and also failed. SDR/BDR teams are still in active hiring across his portfolio. "The AI-replaced-our-GTM-team posts are hype."
- **True AI adoption stays under 1%.** Most marketing teams have done nothing past a GPT prompt or two; shown the output, they like it but won't learn to build it. The technologists who compound value with AI are a tiny fraction; the gap between the two societies widens every quarter.
- **Channels collapse under bot saturation.** When everyone's bot acts in their name, response rates fall across every channel -- the structural ceiling on volume-first automation.
- **SaaS-for-SaaS is risky; tech-for-non-tech is safe.** Build-versus-buy is shifting back to build; niche subscriptions are hard to justify against an hour of vibe-coding. Software sold to other software companies is most exposed (LLMs buy for them, or enterprises build in-house). Tech sold to non-tech buyers (Ramp) stays safe. Raises "are skills the new IP" and where defensibility goes.
- **Be the Olympist of your field.** Not hundreds of millions of Python devs, but a few thousand master-level specialists who can beat the best LLM in a narrow domain. Pick a niche, dedicate ten years (15 to 25), become world-class. The durable human moat when AI commoditises execution.

## Key takeaways

- Empirical hybrid-wins evidence (tested, not asserted) is the spine of [[concept-hybrid-gtm]]; pairs with Mercy Bell's human-infrastructure case ([[framework-three-rs-enablement]]) and Mario's force-multiplier framing.
- "Be the Olympist" + Mario's "taste differentiates" converge into [[concept-specialist-moat]]: depth and taste as the advantage AI cannot commoditise.
- The SaaS-for-SaaS risk thesis sharpens [[concept-borrow-from-engineering]] and the build-the-orchestration-layer move ([[concept-orchestration-layer]]).

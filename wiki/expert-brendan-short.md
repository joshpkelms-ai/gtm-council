# Brendan J Short

> Author of The Signal (thesignal.club), a weekly GTM newsletter for operators and founders (~8,400 readers). Ran Operations & Enablement for Zoom's ~125-person global BDR org in 2020-2021. Writes on scaling GTM with AI and automation; in this corpus he interviews [[expert-mercy-bell]] on the future of enablement.

## Who he is

GTM operator turned newsletter author. The Signal is sponsored by [[tool-clay]] and others. Focus: how to scale GTM with AI and automation without hollowing out the human layer.

## Core positions

- **The best GTM orgs will have the best human infrastructure, not just the best agent infrastructure.** Apply the same rigour used on context windows and evals to ramp, coaching, and leadership systems.
- **Curates practitioner frameworks** rather than only asserting his own; the Renaissance of Enablement piece is built around Mercy Bell's models. See [[framework-three-rs-enablement]].

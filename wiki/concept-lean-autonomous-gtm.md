# Concept: Lean, autonomous, founder-owned GTM

> A business kept deliberately simple so AI and documentation run the operation, a founder brand acquires customers cheaply, and the whole thing stays bootstrapped and near-autonomous past $10M ARR. "Less, smarter, faster, and completely yours."

## The pattern

[[expert-adam-robinson]]'s [[tool-rb2b]] crossed $9M ARR running near-autonomously. The ingredients:

- **Deliberate product simplicity** so AI can handle customer interaction.
- **World-class documentation** so AI support (Intercom Fin) resolves 98.9% of tickets, escalating 1.1%; founders spend minutes a day on support.
- **A founder thought-leadership brand** as a cheap acquisition channel.
- **Cross-subsidised, zero-marginal-cost data** (see [[concept-data-commoditisation]]) to price below competitors' CAC.
- **Bootstrapped, sub-VC-scale ambition** -- a $10-15M lean business is a win, so no need to chase mid-market, add features, or lower churn. Stayed SMB freemium PLG at 9%+ monthly churn.

## Why it works

Simplicity compounds: it enables AI deflection, cheap ops, and a value prop that sticks (RB2B = website ID). Competitors chasing bigger, more complex things vacate the simple, profitable niche.

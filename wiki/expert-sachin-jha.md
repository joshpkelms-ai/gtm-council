# Sachin Jha

> Founder of ONEGTMLab; host of the ONEGTMLab podcast. Works with B2B companies across cyber, DevTools, AI, and healthcare ($1M-$100M ARR) on GTM sequencing. Two big contributions to this corpus: a layered GTM system that insists foundation precedes activation, and a Signal Intelligence Layer that separates intent from signal.

## Who he is

Founder of ONEGTMLab, a GTM consultancy. Runs the ONEGTMLab podcast (Episode 1 with Piyush Agarwal of Reo.Dev). Self-describes as "engineering GTM systems that scale".

## Core positions

- **Build in order: foundation before activation.** Most teams build Layer 3 (outbound, ads, sequences) before finishing Layer 1 (product marketing), so nothing compounds. See [[framework-three-layer-gtm]].
- **Intent data is not signal.** Intent tells you who might be interested; signal tells you who is already moving and why, in a short window. Hoarding signals without a workflow is the common failure. Signal should change how you show up, not just who you target. See [[concept-signal-vs-intent]] and [[framework-signal-intelligence-layer]].
- **Software to see, humans to act.** A GTM intelligence layer will sit before the CRM, prioritising and routing; humans own strategy, narrative, and the signal-to-implication leap that is not automatable yet.
- **PMF before tooling.** Fix ICP, message-market fit, and channel-market fit before layering on signal tools.
- **Clay is the data layer; Claude is the reasoning layer.** A former Clay evangelist, he moves orchestration and reasoning (not the data layer) to Claude Code, arguing "Clay vs Claude" was half-wrong because Clay's AI column already runs Claude. See [[concept-data-vs-reasoning-layer]].

# Andy Toizer

> Head of Growth at Freckle.io. Writes AgentOperator (agentoperator.substack.com) on building GTM with agentic tools. Borrows patterns from software engineers and turns scattered GTM context into agent-ready repos and tools. (Folder spelling: "Andy Tozier"; GitHub handle: Andytoizer.)

## Who he is

Head of Growth at Freckle.io (a GTM product around self-serve signup enrichment, CRM writeback, audience activation, account hygiene, and segmented outbound). Author of the AgentOperator Substack. Ships open-source [[tool-claude-code]] + [[tool-codex]] plugins and tools for GTM engineers.

## Core positions

- **Steal from software engineers; they are ~18 months ahead on coding agents.** See [[concept-borrow-from-engineering]]. Engineers have Context7 (9,000+ libraries of always-current docs agents pull at runtime); the GTM stack was not in it, so he built the GTM equivalent.
- **Make tools agent-ready, with source-backed docs.** See [[tool-gtm-docs-registry]] and [[concept-agent-readiness]]. 184 published GTM tool profiles, each scored /5 on agent readiness across MCP, API, CLI, OpenAPI, llms.txt, and SDK, every claim source-stamped with a verified date.
- **Every campaign should make the next one better.** See [[framework-gtm-brain]]. The loop: AI ships the work, the work creates company learnings, the learnings improve the next output. Better context creates better outputs.
- **Nothing becomes "company truth" silently.** Human judgment is a layer; weekly refreshes propose updates in Slack for approval. Echoes [[concept-claim-tagging]] (approved-claims, product-truth vs aspirational).

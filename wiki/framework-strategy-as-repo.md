# Framework: Strategy as a Repo

> [[expert-jared-waxman]]'s framing: the GTM strategy deliverable moves from decks to version-controlled repos, so research and positioning become assets that compound rather than artifacts that go stale.

## The idea

Strategy used to live in decks. It now lives in repos: a structured set of cross-referencing documents an AI tool, new hire, or agency can read to start smart instead of starting over. Strategy you can diff; research you can trust; marketing that compounds.

## The artifacts (built in dependency order)

1. **Company-context file** -- industry, business model, audiences. The doc every other doc reads first.
2. **ICP** -- two audiences, with pains ranked from actual customer quotes.
3. **Competitor dossiers** -- e.g. six rolled into one threat matrix.
4. **Positioning and messaging library** -- positioning plus a ~10-component messaging library, built in dependency order.
5. **Voice rules** -- extracted from the company's real posts.

## Discipline

Every claim is tagged for provenance: [VERIFIED], [INFERRED], [ESTIMATED], [UNAVAILABLE]. See [[concept-claim-tagging]]. "A blank beats a hallucination, every time."

## Why it matters

The payoff is not speed (a day vs a quarter) but compounding knowledge. Strategy becomes an asset, not an artifact. Pairs naturally with [[tool-claude-code]] skills and repo-based workflows.

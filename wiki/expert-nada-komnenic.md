# Nada Komnenic

> Writes for the HeyReach blog on signal-based outbound. Author of the six-step signal routing engine that sits between a noisy signal stack and LinkedIn execution. (Name as published: Nađa Komnenić.)

## Who she is

A HeyReach contributor (see [[tool-heyreach]]). One source here, the practical blueprint for routing buyer-intent signals safely into LinkedIn campaigns.

## Core positions

- **An orchestration problem, not a signal problem.** Funding alerts, job changes, tech installs, enrichment updates, and CRM scores all look like high intent; together they are noise. Without a logic layer you get duplicate outreach, conflicting messages, overloaded sender seats, and account risk.
- **The six-step routing engine.** Before any signal reaches a sender: Validate, Dedupe, Prioritise, Route, Pace, Monitor. Pacing protects sender health; Monitor catches the system quietly failing at scale. See [[framework-sales-orchestration]] and [[concept-silent-failures]].

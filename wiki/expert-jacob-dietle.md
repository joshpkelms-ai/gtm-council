# Jacob Dietle

> Founder of Taste Systems, a one-person context-engineering consultancy that helps companies (mostly on the GTM side) build a "context OS" so AI moves faster and ships higher-quality work. Came to GTM from an information-systems background via web design, workflow automation, then GTM engineering.

## Who he is

A self-described context engineer ("context ops"). Works as a solo consultancy inside a network of consultants, coming into a company, finding the top two or three GTM use cases (often inbound/content first), and building out the context system that powers them. Clients include Pixee (application security) and others. Publishes a body of resources on building context engines.

## Core positions

- **Context is information that changes the meaning of other information.** AI is fact-rich and context-poor; it amplifies whatever you give it and otherwise defaults to the lowest common denominator. The job is to systematically supply context toward a goal. See [[concept-context-assembly]].
- **Context ops is a new function** -- the GTM analogue of DevOps. Someone (a "benevolent dictator", a subject-matter expert) must own and maintain the company's canonical context.
- **Foundation before scope.** Build a small house on a strong foundation. Find the context bottleneck (no ICP and it hallucinates) and pick three starting use cases. Echoes [[concept-foundations-before-automation]] and [[framework-three-layer-gtm]].
- **Back-pressure beats hope.** Iterate from ~60% to ~85% by decomposing a great example into testable building blocks and feeding the missing context each round; the GTM substitute for software's unit tests. Links to [[concept-borrow-from-engineering]].
- **Amplification, not replacement.** The 10-20% AI cannot reach is your intuition, your competitive edge, "stamped into the machine". Beware the curse of knowledge. Pairs with [[concept-specialist-moat]] and [[concept-hybrid-gtm]].
- **Build and buy.** Build your own deep workspace for customisation and understanding; buy an opinionated tool like [[tool-octave]] for multiplayer and non-technical reach. Do both.

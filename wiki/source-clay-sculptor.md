# Source: Clay -- Stop Guessing, Start Analyzing (Sculptor)

> A Clay product post introducing Sculptor, Clay's conversational AI copilot for analysing GTM data in natural language. Distilled into the Sculptor section of [[tool-clay]]. Raw: `raw/Stop Guessing, Start Analyzing...md`.

## Summary

GTM data is fragmented (customers in Snowflake, closed-lost in Salesforce, inbound through forms), and extracting intelligence from it normally needs technical expertise and waiting on data teams, often until the opportunity has passed. **Sculptor** is Clay's conversational AI copilot that understands full GTM-workflow context and answers questions over connected data (Salesforce accounts enriched with Mixpanel usage, Snowflake lists, inbound leads with buying signals) in natural language, no SQL, no waiting.

Three tactical workflows:

1. **Define your ICP with actual customer data.** Bring CRM/warehouse data into Clay, enrich it (employee count, HQ, tech stack), send back to Snowflake, then ask Sculptor to break down customers by employee count, revenue, deal size, credits spent, and champion personas/titles/seniority, and to surface common traits and recommend Clay search filters to find lookalikes.
2. (and further use cases) analysing and iterating on GTM data conversationally, framed as "a conversation with your data" that adapts to business context, stack, and goals.

## Why it matters here

Sculptor is Clay pushing into the **reasoning/analysis layer** on top of its data layer: it is the in-Clay expression of [[concept-data-vs-reasoning-layer]] (Clay does breadth/accuracy; an LLM copilot does the depth/judgment), and a self-serve answer to the RevOps reporting bottleneck. The ICP-from-real-customers workflow operationalises [[framework-signal-intelligence-layer]] and lookalike targeting, and pairs with [[concept-list-is-the-message]] (let the data define the segment).

## Key takeaways

- Sculptor = natural-language analysis of connected GTM data inside Clay; no SQL, no data-team queue.
- Flagship use: derive a real ICP from closed-won/active customers and get recommended Clay filters for lookalikes.
- Clay moving up the stack from data into conversational reasoning over that data.

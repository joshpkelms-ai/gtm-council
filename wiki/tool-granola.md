# Tool: Granola

> AI meeting-notes tool whose transcripts feed downstream GTM skills, and whose MCP turns every meeting into queryable GTM context.

## What it is

A meeting recorder / note-taker. In the corpus it appears two ways: as the transcript source for structured-extraction skills, and (per the GTM Engineer Pulse) as a "Favourite MCP" that turns every meeting into queryable GTM context, fitting the company-brain and context-assembly patterns ([[framework-ai-employee-loop]], [[concept-context-assembly]]).

## Position in the corpus

[[expert-matteo-tittarelli]] feeds Granola transcripts into his case-study extraction skill to produce a sourced draft in about a minute (see [[play-case-study-extraction]]). The risk he flags is decay: a transcript fresh in week 1 is buried by week 4, so the extraction should run at the moment of close. Note the parallel with [[expert-eric-nowoslawski]]'s use of Fathom recordings as a context source in [[framework-ai-employee-loop]] -- meeting transcripts are a primary feedstock for GTM context repos.

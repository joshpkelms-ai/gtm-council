# Wesley Hoang

> Cofounder of Cymate and AgencyWorkplaces, building a B2B software growth firm and outbound agency. Practical on two fronts: natural-language lead-list building via MCP, and a disciplined cold-email copy testing sequence.

## Who he is

Cofounder of Cymate (cymate.io) and AgencyWorkplaces. Runs an outbound agency for B2B software companies and tech startups.

## Core positions

- **Describe your ICP, let AI build the list.** See [[play-natural-language-icp-list-build]] and [[concept-agentic-sourcing]]. Connecting [[tool-claude-code]] to [[tool-ai-ark]] via [[tool-mcp]] replaces filters and dropdowns with a plain-English request: "Grab me 50 CEOs in New York running SaaS companies doing one to five million ARR." Claude runs the search, pulls data, exports a CSV with emails and LinkedIn profiles, then scores and flags leads worth reaching out to first.
- **Test the big things, optimise the small ones.** See [[framework-cold-email-copy-testing]]. Founders test 50 variations at once and learn nothing, optimise subject lines before validating the core message, and never isolate the offer, which is the one variable that decides replies. Test angle then structure then CTA then (rarely) subject line. If no replies, change the offer, not the email.

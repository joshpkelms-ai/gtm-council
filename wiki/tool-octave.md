# Tool: Octave

> An opinionated, off-the-shelf context OS built for go-to-market: a structured company knowledge graph (positioning, personas, pain points) wired to GTM tools, the "buy" option in the build-vs-buy of context engineering.

## What it is

Octave handles both the strategic context (everything about your company) and dynamic context (fetch the intent signal at this point in time), ships a solid go-to-market knowledge graph out of the box, and connects to GTM tools so context pushes through to where reps work. In the corpus it is the canonical "buy" answer to the question every context-engineering team hits: build your own context system or buy one.

## Position in the corpus

- **Buy for multiplayer and non-technical reach.** [[expert-jacob-dietle]]'s recommendation: use Octave when you have many people, especially reps and traditional marketers who will not live in [[tool-claude-code]] but need context in their regular AI. It is opinionated, the integrations are there, and there is far less up-front config. See [[source-jacob-dietle-context-engineering]].
- **Complement, not replacement.** Dietle advises doing both: buy Octave for breadth and reach, build your own deep workspace for customisation, the long tail of business-specific use cases, and the understanding that makes the bought tool more powerful. The general trade-off is customisation (power, but setup cost) versus opinionated defaults (speed, but limits).
- **A likely category.** Expect "the Octave for X" verticals as context-OS tooling matures.

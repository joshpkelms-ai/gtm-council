# Source: Matteo Tittarelli -- Optimise for AI citations (Framer AEO Scanner)

> LinkedIn post by [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]] (Framer brand partnership). Raw: `raw/Matteo Titt/4.md`. Anchor source for [[concept-aeo]].

## Summary

More buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini before they touch Google; some never reach Google at all. The lost traffic does not show in GA: no bounce rate to fix, no keyword to chase, just a slow leak of pipeline to whoever the AI cited this week. Most sites Matteo audits read well to humans but look unstructured to an LLM: sloppy HTML, missing metadata, slow pages, no schema, so LLMs cannot read them and AI does not cite them. Almost nobody optimises for AI citations yet, so the opportunity gap is wide but closing.

He points to [[tool-framer]]'s free AEO Scanner: paste a URL to get an AEO score, the specific things hurting visibility, and a fix plan (missing schema, broken header hierarchy, unparseable content); it works on any site. Framer can ship it because its pages are AEO-ready by default (clean HTML, fast loads, structured metadata, auto-updating sitemaps). The example report for genesysgrowth.com scored 73/100 (Findable and Quotable good, Understandable average, Trustworthy poor; internal links missing).

## Key takeaways

- Answer-engine optimisation (AEO) is the emerging discipline: structure pages so LLMs can parse and cite them.
- AI-referred traffic is invisible in standard analytics, which hides the loss.
- Scored dimensions in the example tool: Findable, Quotable, Understandable, Trustworthy.
- "The teams getting cited by AI in 2027 are the ones taking action in 2026."

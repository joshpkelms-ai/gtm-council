# Tool: RB2B

> Person-level website visitor identification. Identifies 70-80% of website traffic at the individual level. Built and run by [[expert-adam-robinson]] (also Retention.com).

## What it is

A website visitor de-anonymisation product: RB2B = website ID. It tells you which individuals visited your site, turning anonymous traffic into named contacts for inbound and signal-based outbound.

## Position in the corpus

Less a tactic, more a case study in [[concept-lean-autonomous-gtm]]: RB2B crossed $9M ARR running near-autonomously, kept deliberately simple so AI (Intercom Fin at 98.9% ticket resolution) handles support, acquired through [[expert-adam-robinson]]'s founder brand, and priced below competitors' CAC on the back of a cross-subsidised, zero-marginal-cost data asset (see [[concept-data-commoditisation]]). Stayed SMB freemium PLG at 9%+ monthly churn rather than chasing mid-market.

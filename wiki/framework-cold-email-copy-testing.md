# Framework: Cold Email Copy Testing

> [[expert-wesley-hoang]]'s winning-copy sequence: isolate one variable per round and test the big things (offer, angle) before optimising the small things (subject line).

## The principle

Test, do not optimise prematurely. Testing 50 variations at once teaches nothing. The offer is the single variable that most determines replies, so isolate it. Subject lines and send times are optimisation, not testing.

## The sequence

1. **Angle** -- test 2-3 angles with 2 templates. Pick the winning angle.
2. **Structure** -- test 3 copy structures with the winning angle. Pick the winning structure.
3. **CTA** -- test 3 CTAs with the winning angle and structure. Pick the winning CTA.
4. **Subject line** -- usually not needed; optimise only if warranted.
5. **Output** -- best-performing copy, then scale.

## The offer rule

If you are not getting replies, do not rewrite the email; change what you offer at the end. Demo not landing? Try a case study, a quick call, or a coffee chat. Once something hits, optimise, then scale.

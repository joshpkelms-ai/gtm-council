# Mario Moscatiello

> VP of Marketing at [[tool-airbyte]] (the open-source data integration platform). Came up the dev-tools growth path (Pusher, GitBook, then Principal at Flex Capital and board observer at Strapi, Head of Growth at FluteStack), advising Airbyte for four years before joining full-time. Built a warm-outbound motion triangulating GitHub, product-usage, and pricing-page signals that doubled Airbyte's pipeline growth rate. Guest on the GTM Engineer School podcast (S2E5), hosted by [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]]. (The "Mario" referenced in Matteo's Airbyte Context Store post.)

## Core positions

- **Foundations before automation.** GTM engineering is a step change only once product-market fit exists and revops data is clean. AI is a multiplier of whatever foundation exists -- including garbage. The two foundations are the PMM hat (personas, competitor, market, what works) and the revops hat (clean fields, enrichments, attribution). Anchors [[concept-foundations-before-automation]]; same thesis as [[framework-three-layer-gtm]].
- **Force multiplier per role, not headcount replacement.** Every team member can now own a workflow idea-to-live in an hour (paid SEO; an SDR manager from list to launched campaign). Five strong people doing the work of fifteen, not one star doing the work of ten (key-man risk). Notes Anthropic and OpenAI hire engineers AND AEs while shipping tools that supposedly replace both. Cf. [[concept-hybrid-gtm]].
- **Warm outbound is signal triangulation, not message volume.** Two plays drove the pipeline doubling. (1) Events: dump post-event leads into [[tool-common-room]], rank by product signup / open-source repo use, SDRs only call the warm subset. (2) PLG signups: when an engineer signs up, reach out to them, prospect decision-makers in the same org, and warm those decision-makers with ads before the SDR call (Twilio "Ask Your Developer" at scale). "Even when a motion has to be cold, the question is how to warm it up."
- **Hire barrels, not ammunition; then outsource deep expertise.** Hire generalists who take a project end-to-end without a playbook (his best SDR sold pest control door-to-door). Four traits: agency, curiosity, taste, chip on the shoulder. With AI driving the cost of copy and code to zero, taste is the differentiator -- the human side of [[concept-specialist-moat]]. Complement the team with agencies (deliverability, paid media, scaled outbound) so it manages agents and workflows, not CPMs.
- **Stop doom-scrolling, start listening.** The one habit to start: a Gong digest of what customers actually say, over copying what other people post.

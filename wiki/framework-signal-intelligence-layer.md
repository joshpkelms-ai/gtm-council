# Framework: The GTM Signal Intelligence Layer

> [[expert-sachin-jha]]'s model for sorting signals into three tiers and matching your motion to the tier, instead of hoarding every signal and acting on none. Open-sourced as a Figma file and a scoring sheet.

## The three tiers

- **Layer 1 -- Early Intent (market awareness).** High-volume, low-intent: social engagement on problem-centric posts, blog scroll depth, newsletter subscriptions, community questions, research-level keyword searches. Tells you someone is aware of a problem, not buying. Narrative-shaping territory, not outbound.
- **Layer 2 -- Mid Intent (evaluation mode).** Context-rich, shortlist-building: pricing-page views, case-study visits, decision-makers posting buying-context content on LinkedIn, job posts mentioning tools, stack changes, competitor ad exposure, review comparisons. These people are moving and in-market.
- **Layer 3 -- High Intent (GTM-ready).** Low-volume, execution-ready: demo-page visits, integration-doc views, security/pricing FAQ engagement, contact-intent pages, funding announcements, sales-leadership hires, tool-implementation mentions, migration indicators. Act fast; this is where teams drop the ball as the window closes.

## How to use it

Match the motion to the tier: don't send a hard CTA to someone browsing community forums, and don't send a thought-leadership piece to someone already on your pricing page. The aim is to prioritise which signals matter for *your* motion rather than collecting all of them.

## Grounding ideas

- Built on the [[concept-signal-vs-intent]] distinction: signal (who's moving now, and why) over intent (who might be interested).
- Signal should change *how* you show up, not just *who* you target -- it rewrites the opening line and the questions you ask. Connects to [[concept-list-is-the-message]].
- In DevTools, traditional intent breaks (anonymous installs, no form fills); behavioural signals (installs, deployments, doc engagement, open-source activity) are the raw material. The signal-to-implication leap stays human ("not automatable yet").
- Sits before the CRM as an intelligence layer that tells downstream tools what to do; pairs with Layer 3 of [[framework-three-layer-gtm]].

# Framework: The 3-Layer GTM System

> [[expert-sachin-jha]]'s (ONEGTMLab) model: GTM has three layers that must be built in order -- Product Marketing, then Context Engineering, then GTM Engineering. Build a later layer on a missing earlier one and it amplifies garbage. "You cannot put walls up before you pour the foundation."

## The three layers

1. **Layer 1 -- Product Marketing (the core / foundation).** ICP research, positioning, messaging, value prop, buyer personas, competitive and win/loss analysis, JTBD map, pain-point framework, GTM playbooks, pricing narrative, core story doc. *Without this: every asset, campaign, and sequence is a guess.*
2. **Layer 2 -- Context Engineering (the amplifier).** Takes the PMM foundation and feeds it into AI systems for quality at 10x volume: AI context files, prompt templates, brand-voice profiles, competitor-intel packs, ICP briefs, asset templates, tone calibration, knowledge-base sync, QA/feedback loops, workflow automation. *Without this: AI produces generic slop, no brand consistency, no taste.* This is [[concept-context-assembly]] as a named layer.
3. **Layer 3 -- GTM Engineering (the activator).** Turns assets into pipeline: signal detection, outbound sequences, email campaigns, lead-scoring AI, SDR/persona routing, LinkedIn outreach, retargeting ads, multi-channel plays, pipeline acceleration. *Without this: great assets sit in a Drive folder, zero pipeline.*

## Why sequence matters

Layer 3 shows results fast and feeds the busy-feeling that lies (activity without compounding). Layer 1 is slow, has no dashboard, and gets skipped or done in one forgettable afternoon. Teams then build Layer 3 on a shaky foundation and wonder why nothing compounds, often returning after 6-9 months of doing the same things harder. The funnel isn't broken; the starting point is.

## The asset map

Each funnel stage (Awareness/Consideration/Purchase/Retention) has assets in all three columns -- e.g. Awareness: buyer-persona docs (PMM) -> AI blog posts at scale (Context) -> LinkedIn outreach on warm signals (GTM Eng); Retention: expansion narrative (PMM) -> CS scripts (Context) -> churn-risk alert workflows (GTM Eng). Nothing works in isolation; every piece connects.

## Diagnostic

Layer 1: can everyone give the *same* one-sentence "why us"? Is the ICP written with segment/persona/use-case? Is the narrative one sales actually uses? Layer 2: does AI output sound like your company? Are there documented context files and a QA loop? Layer 3: are signals mapped to ICP or are you tracking everything and acting on nothing? Is personalisation informed by Layer 1 or a first-name swap?

# Jared Waxman

> Founder of GTM Engineer School (AI automation training and placement for GTM teams, using Clay and [[tool-claude-code]]). Operating Partner at Unusual Ventures. Best known for treating GTM strategy as version-controlled assets that live in repos.

## Who he is

Founder of GTM Engineer School, which trains and places GTM engineers in AI automation ([[tool-clay]], [[tool-claude-code]] and similar). He co-founded the school with [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]], whom he met in the HyperGrowth Partners operator community. Operating Partner at Unusual Ventures, so he runs working sessions with portfolio companies. Teaches cohorts; Cohort 4 (May 25 - July 14) ran a project-based "muse company" model -- see [[source-matteo-tittarelli-gtm-engineer-school-cohort4]].

## Core positions

- **Strategy lives in repos, not decks.** See [[framework-strategy-as-repo]]. A working session for a portfolio company produced a company-context file, a two-audience ICP ranked from real customer quotes, six competitor dossiers in one threat matrix, positioning and a 10-component messaging library built in dependency order, and voice rules extracted from real posts.
- **Tag every claim.** Each claim is marked [VERIFIED], [INFERRED], [ESTIMATED], or [UNAVAILABLE]. See [[concept-claim-tagging]]. "A blank beats a hallucination, every time."
- **Strategy is an asset, not an artifact.** The payoff is not speed but compounding: the next AI tool, hire, or agency reads the repo and starts smart instead of starting over. Strategy you can diff.
- **Know how skill namespacing works before you trust it.** See [[concept-skill-namespacing]]. Claude Code skills silently shadow each other across personal, project, and plugin scopes.

# Source: Matteo Tittarelli -- Four years, 61 clients, a compounding channel stack

> LinkedIn post by [[expert-matteo-tittarelli]]. Raw: `raw/Matteo Titt/2.md`. Anchor source for [[concept-compounding-channel-stack]].

## Summary

After four years and 61 clients as a solopreneur, his answer to "how do you get clients" is a stack of channels whose mix reshaped every year:

- **Year 1: ~85% personal network.** Almost every client was someone he had worked with before going solo. No audience, no system, just existing relationships.
- **Year 2: ~55% network, ~30% referrals.** People he knew started recommending him; referrals compounded on top of the network.
- **Year 3: ~40% LinkedIn, ~35% referrals.** Two years of showing up turned into revenue; for the first time his biggest channel was one he owned outright.
- **Year 4: ~40% LinkedIn, ~25% HyperGrowth.** HyperGrowth Partners (Guillaume Cabane and Martin Gontovnikas's operator community) became his highest-quality source (Common Room, Strapi, AirOps), and is where he met [[expert-jared-waxman]] and co-founded GTM Engineer School.

His attached dashboard breaks revenue by channel and by service lane (PMM leads lifetime value; AI Transformation the fastest-emerging lane). Where he would start over: list everyone you loved working with; post on LinkedIn and X from day one; use that brand to get into an operator community fast. Run together they compound -- it took him four years to stack all three; you could do it in one.

## Key takeaways

- Client acquisition compounds: network starts you, an owned audience opens doors, the right community fills them with better clients than you would find alone.
- Invest earliest in the channels you own (audience) and the rooms you earn into (community).
- One community (HyperGrowth) produced great clients, a co-founder, and the push to build his own community.

# Kieran Flanagan

> Senior GTM leader (CMO background, scaled marketing at HubSpot and Zapier) who writes on AI and go-to-market. In this corpus, the practitioner who applies the LLM-wiki pattern to GTM *leadership*: an Obsidian + Claude Code "Second Brain" that changed how he makes decisions running a 400+ person org.

## Who he is

A long-tenured GTM/marketing executive writing at kieranflanagan.io. The one source here is his account of building an AI Second Brain before stepping into a new role leading 400+ people, to compress the brutal first-few-months information gauntlet.

## Core positions

- **A vault plus an agent, not either alone.** "A vault without AI is a wiki that decays because no one has time to maintain it. AI without a vault starts from zero every session." The system is an Obsidian vault (structured knowledge) plus [[tool-claude-code]] (the intelligence that reads, writes, and reasons across it). A worked GTM-leadership instance of [[concept-llm-wiki-pattern]].
- **CLAUDE.md is the constitution.** Identity, voice, non-negotiables, conventions; auto-loaded every session alongside the project index and recent daily logs, so the agent has full strategic context before you speak. Co-evolved over time. Echoes [[framework-strategy-as-repo]] and [[framework-gtm-brain]].
- **Persistence is the unlock.** What separates it from dropping a question into a chat is continuity: the system remembers, compounds, and sharpens with use.

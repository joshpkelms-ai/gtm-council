# Brandon Camhi

> Joined Rippling in November 2019 (~150 employees, $10M ARR) as the first growth marketing IC; built the growth team and led all of marketing through nearly $1B ARR. Now COO at Netic (a Founders Fund and Greylock-backed AI revenue engine for essential services), building a modern GTM engine from scratch with [[expert-noah-adelstein]].

## Who he is

A marketing leader who scaled one B2B engine to near-$1B and is now rebuilding from a fresh slate with AI at his disposal. Went from leading a ~200-person org at Rippling to personally coding marketing assets at Netic.

## Core positions

- **Alpha is in timeless marketing principles, not the tooling.** The tactical playbook (events, signals, storytelling) is broadly the same; AI raises the "permission to play" quality bar. Returns accrue to teams that tell incredible stories, not to whoever has the best agent.
- **Hire spiky mutants, not generalists.** "A small team of mutants who each are spiky in one or more areas" beats a larger team; each person raises the bar on a different dimension. The human-side input to [[concept-specialist-moat]].
- **Know where to seek alpha and where to do no harm.** Build secret sauce in some dimensions (e.g. a creative TAM build); choose predictable reliability in others (Netic picked Salesforce over an AI-native CRM). 
- **Earn the right to automate; AI is a means to an end.** "I would take a totally analog GTM org that produces hyper-relevant things over a fully automated one that isn't working." See [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]].
- **Every exec must be in the tools.** Leaders should get hands-on rather than delegate three levels down.

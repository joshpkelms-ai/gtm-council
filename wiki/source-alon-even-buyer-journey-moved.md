# Source: Alon Even -- The buyer journey moved. Your GTM didn't.

> Substack essay by [[expert-alon-even]]. Raw: `raw/Clippings/The buyer journey moved. Your GTM didn't..md`. Anchors [[concept-ai-first-buyer-journey]].

## Summary

A client was happy with opportunities from branded search; talking to buyers revealed the truth: they found the company on ChatGPT first, Googled the brand to check legitimacy, then converted. The campaign didn't drive discovery; AI did, and Google was just verification. The buyer journey didn't disappear, it moved upstream into places the GTM system wasn't built to see.

- **The journey you're not tracking** happens in AI conversations, private Slack/executive networks, and dark social, before pipeline. Stats cited: 51% of B2B software buyers now begin research in an AI chatbot (up from 29% a year prior, G2 Mar 2026); 72% start with peer recommendations in private groups (Wynter 2025); 92% enter formal evaluation with at least one vendor already in mind (Forrester 2025); 70% of B2B tech queries trigger AI Overviews, most with no click-out (Stackmatix 2026).
- **Where signal lives (three places):** peer / dark social signal (trusted, invisible to attribution, weighs most); AI signal (what ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Mode surface, learned from expert content, research, reviews, community); verification signal (site, LinkedIn, customer proof, confirms rather than creates interest).
- **AEO is necessary but incomplete.** Optimising for AI Overviews (schema, citation-friendly content, structured data) isn't wrong, but "you can't earn a presence inside a journey that already happened without you" if there's no real signal behind it. See [[concept-aeo]].

## What it means for the GTM system

Two things, in order: (1) start with how and why buyers actually buy -- the real process (who's in the room, what triggers the search, who they ask first, where they verify, what they already hear about your category), not a persona deck; (2) build a system that moves when buyers do, watching how the journey changes and updating where signals get created, rather than chasing every new tool. "AI didn't change what builds trust. It changed where trust is encountered, and how fast your GTM system needs to adapt."

## Between the lines: one compounding system

Brand is underfunded, demand over-engineered, retention/expansion treated as someone else's job, and that imbalance kills signal compounding. Signal is created by the full system: brand builds memory before buyers are in-market, demand captures intent, retention/expansion create the proof that makes the next buyer trust faster. Customer proof, advocacy, expansion stories, and referrals are signal, not just post-sale activities. "One GTM system that compounds, or three motions that leak." Connects to [[concept-compounding-channel-stack]].

## Key takeaways

- Presence must exist upstream and early, where AI and peers shape preference before any search.
- AEO without genuine signal behind it is hollow; pairs with [[concept-aeo]] and [[concept-signal-vs-intent]].

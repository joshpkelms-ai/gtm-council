# Noah Adelstein

> Author of the gtm engineer (thegtmengineer.substack.com) and founder of The GTM Engineer Lab. Early user of [[tool-clay]], HeyReach, PhantomBuster. Built growth at Hearth and Rippling (Head of International and Automated Growth under [[expert-brandon-camhi]]); now leads Growth at Netic, where he and Camhi are building a modern GTM engine from scratch.

## Who he is

GTM engineer and operator who runs an experiment-led newsletter: try new tools, document what works, what doesn't, and what is really going on. His thesis: GTM alpha comes primarily from ideas, software, and workflows that others have not found yet, so try new tools often.

## Core positions

- **Earn the right to automate.** Prove a motion works manually before scaling it with AI; "go prove the ceiling is sufficiently high before you think about all the automation". If a single hand-written email never got a positive reply, do not send 100,000. See [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]].
- **Humans generate the context AI later codifies.** Dedicated full-time humans accumulate market context far faster than a GTM engineer splitting time across eight projects. The SDR is a context generator; the GTME codifies the proven playbook.
- **Adoption is the prioritisation signal.** Of infinite workflows to build, let real usage tell you which deserve engineering investment. See [[concept-adoption-as-prioritisation]] and the Vanta/Dust experiment.

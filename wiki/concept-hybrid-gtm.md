# Concept: Hybrid GTM beats full automation

> [[expert-guillaume-cabane]] (HyperGrowth Partners), the recognised godfather of GTM engineering: human+AI hybrid teams beat full automation, and he has the test to back it. The corpus's strongest empirical counterweight to the "AI replaced our SDR team" narrative.

## The evidence

Cabane tested full automation against a human+AI hybrid setup at Ramp himself -- and failed to beat the hybrid baseline. Others brought him their prompts to try to beat it and also failed. Across his portfolio (Ramp, Neon, Zapier, AirOps, n8n, Attention) SDR/BDR teams are still in active hiring. He has yet to see a single scale-up operate without a GTM team. The "AI-replaced-our-GTM-team" posts are hype.

## Why full automation hits a ceiling

- **Channel collapse under bot saturation.** When everyone's bot acts in its owner's name, response rates fall across every channel -- volume-first automation erodes its own returns.
- **Adoption is tiny.** True AI adoption (building, not prompting) stays under ~1% of the population; most teams stop at a GPT prompt or two. The leverage is real but concentrated.

## The shape of the win

Not one star doing the work of ten (key-man risk), but a force multiplier per role -- "five strong people doing the work of fifteen" ([[expert-mario-moscatiello]]). The team manages agents and workflows and outsources deep commodity expertise (deliverability, paid media). The human contribution is judgment, taste, and relationship -- which is why the durable advantage is [[concept-specialist-moat]] (be the Olympist of your field).

## Where it sits in the corpus

The counterweight to over-reading [[concept-lean-autonomous-gtm]] and [[concept-borrow-from-engineering]]: steal the engineering patterns, but keep the human in the loop. Pairs with [[expert-mercy-bell]]'s human-infrastructure case ([[framework-three-rs-enablement]]) -- both argue the people layer survives and matters alongside the agent layer. [[expert-brandon-camhi]] and [[expert-noah-adelstein]] at Netic give the build-from-scratch version: "an engine with humans at the centre", AI as a means to an end, with [[concept-earn-the-right-to-automate]] as the operating discipline.

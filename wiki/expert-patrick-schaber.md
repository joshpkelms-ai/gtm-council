# Patrick Schaber

> Fractional AI and marketing leader writing Approachable AI by Pat Schaber (patrickschaber.substack.com); helps small and mid-sized businesses transform GTM operations with AI. His angle is accessibility: practical, minutes-to-implement workflows for sales and marketing teams without an engineering function.

## Who he is

Runs advisory engagements (Quick Win sessions, AI Readiness Assessments, fractional AI/marketing leadership). Documents his own stack openly. Best known in this corpus for the [[tool-clay]]-inside-[[tool-claude-code]] (Cowork) integration walkthrough.

## Core positions

- **Build an operating system in Claude, then prompt against it.** His pre-configured Cowork "operating system" already holds his business context, ICP, positioning, and messaging, so a one-line prompt is enough; Claude asks targeted follow-ups and drafts on-brand outreach with little instruction. A practical instance of context-as-foundation; cf. [[framework-gtm-brain]] and [[concept-context-assembly]].
- **The Clay-in-Claude integration is the unlock for non-engineers.** Clay data renders cleanly inside Claude: contact cards (info, LinkedIn, work-history summary), company tabs, hundreds of enrichable data points. Claude tiers and scores prospects against the stored ICP, spins up multiple agents to score lists, drafts emails into Gmail drafts and LinkedIn notes. See [[concept-data-vs-reasoning-layer]].
- **Always edit and verify before sending.** "I would never simply send these" -- a human verification step on AI-drafted outreach. A lightweight echo of [[concept-claim-tagging]] discipline.
- **AI evens the GTM playing field.** Work that took multiple meetings, heavy research time and budget can now run in hours on minimal spend, putting small teams on near-equal footing with larger ones.
